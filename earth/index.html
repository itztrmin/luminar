About This Project

Earth,
rendered true

TERRA is a WebGL-powered Earth visualization built with Three.js and driven by NASA texture data. Every pixel of the surface — from the Sahara's ochre to the Amazon's green — is sourced from real satellite imagery.

The goal was singular: make it feel real. Not a diagram, not a toy globe — but something that makes you hold your breath for a moment.

Sphere Segments 128 × 128
Rendering ACES Filmic
Texture Resolution 8192 × 4096
Shader Language GLSL ES 3.0
